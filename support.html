JaanchLo help and contact

Being scammed right now, or already lost money? Hang up and call 1930, the National Cyber Crime Helpline, then file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Call your bank on the number printed on your card to block it. JaanchLo cannot recover money or contact the police for you.

Report a wrong verdict

If JaanchLo called a scam safe, or a genuine message dangerous, please tell us. Remove any personal details first, then send the message text and the verdict to romil@regnor.systems, or open an issue on GitHub.

Using JaanchLo in ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT something like "Is this message a scam?" and paste the message, or describe a suspicious call. You can ask for the answer in Hindi. If the JaanchLo tools stop responding, email us and we will look into it.

Report a scam number

To report a fraud call, SMS or WhatsApp message even when no money was lost, use Chakshu on Sanchar Saathi.

Contact

Email romil@regnor.systems. We usually reply within two working days.
