1. What JaanchLo is

JaanchLo is a free, open-source tool that checks the text of messages, and the details of calls, for patterns common in scams in India. It returns a verdict (Safe, Be careful, or Danger), reasons, and suggested next steps. It is provided by Regnor as a public-good project, free of charge.

2. A second opinion, not a guarantee

JaanchLo uses fixed rules. It can miss a new kind of scam, and it can flag a genuine message. A "Safe" verdict does not mean a message is safe to act on, and a "Danger" verdict is not a legal finding about any person. JaanchLo is not legal, financial or police advice. When in doubt, do not pay, do not share an OTP, and call the National Cyber Crime Helpline on 1930.
